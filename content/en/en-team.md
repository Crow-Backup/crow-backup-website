Crow Backup is currently being developed in Lucerne (Switzerland) by Laurin, Roman, Raphael and Lina. Laurin and Roman have a bachelor’s degree in software development, Lina in graphic design and Raphael in business informatics. And they have all been working successfully in their respective professions for several years.

{{< profile name="Laurin Murer" image="/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Laurin-Murer-1024x1024.jpeg" >}}
Luzern, CH

As one of the two main developers of Crow Backup, I am responsible for ensuring that your data is stored securely and is not lost. I primarily develop the technology in the background (backend) and make sure that, in the worst case scenario, your data can be restored at any time (provided your friend is online). When I’m not developing, I like to spend time with my familiy or go travelling.
