An effective method to protect yourself from such disasters is the 3-2-1 backup rule. In this article, we will explore the 3-2-1 backup strategy in conjunction with the innovative Crow Backup solution.

## Backup at friends

The idea of backing up your data on your friends‘ computers may seem unconventional, but it has considerable advantages:

1.  **Free storage space:** The storage space for your backup is free of charge as it is provided by friends.
2.  **Trusted place:** Your data is in a place you can trust – in your friends‘ network.
3.  **Local recovery:** Your data can be conveniently restored via the local network, saving time and bandwidth.

## The 3-2-1 backup rule in context

The 3-2-1 backup rule is a proven method to ensure that your data is optimally protected. Here is a compact summary:

-   **3:** Three copies of your data – the original and two backup copies.
-   **2:** Use of two different media types – for example, local hard drives and cloud storage.
-   **1:** A copy outside the current location – in our case, on the computers of your friends.

Implementing the 3-2-1 rule with Crow Backup enables efficient data backup. In addition to your main device, you can use cloud storage and external hard drives, while your friends provide a secure external storage location.

The importance of regular data backups is emphasised by alarming statistics. In 2019 alone, data loss in German companies resulted in damage totalling over 100 billion euros. Over 60 per cent of private users have also been affected by data loss.
