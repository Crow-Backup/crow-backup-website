At a time when more and more data is being generated and the need for reliable backups is growing, it is crucial to find a solution that is both secure and accessible. Crow Backup offers just that: an intuitive platform for archiving encrypted copies based on the storage space friends already have.

## Security and data protection first and foremost

Crow Backup’s philosophy is simple: utilise the existing storage space that is already spread across the computers of many households. This avoids storing sensitive data overseas with big tech companies. With Crow Backup, your data is backed up on the computers of trusted friends, which not only means free storage space, but also ensures the highest level of trust and security. You always know which data is backed up and with whom.

All backed-up data is encrypted to the highest security standards (AES-256). Neither the Crow Backup team nor your friends have access to your data. This means that you retain complete control over your private information.

## Simplicity and user-friendliness

An intuitive and simple user interface is a key element of Crow Backup. It’s important to us that the backup process is straightforward and accessible so that anyone can back up their data without any technical expertise. With Crow Backup, you can back up and restore your data with just a few clicks, without having to make complicated settings.

## Friend-based data backup

The idea of using friends‘ storage space offers many advantages. In addition to the free storage space, users can be sure that their data is backed up in a trusted and known location. Data can even be restored via the local network, saving time and bandwidth. But it is also an ecologically sensible alternative: thanks to Crow Backup, it is possible to utilise the storage space that is already available on countless home computers – without encouraging the expansion of resource-intensive server farms!

## Conclusion

Crow Backup is the ideal solution for those seeking security, privacy and accessibility for their backups. With strong encryption, a user-friendly interface and the ability to share storage space with friends, Crow Backup offers a unique combination of security, trust and sensible use of existing resources. Protect your data and rely on Crow Backup – the secure and freely accessible solution for your data backup.
