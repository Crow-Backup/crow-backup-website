In our modern world, where digital data is becoming increasingly important, it is essential to ensure that your valuable information is kept safe and secure. While many people utilise cloud storage solutions, there is a new way to back up your data while ensuring a special level of security and trust: Crow Backup. In this article, we explain why it might be a good idea to store your backups with trusted friends instead of in the cloud.

## Why should you consider Crow Backup?

Crow Backup is a simple software that allows you to store backups of your important data on the computers of your close friends. This approach offers several advantages over traditional methods of data backup, such as cloud storage.

## Your data remains in good hands

When you use Crow Backup, your data stays in trustworthy hands. Your friends help you to store your data securely. Unlike the cloud, where your data is stored on the servers of large companies, with Crow Backup you have full control over where your backups are stored.

## Protection through strong encryption

Crow Backup uses strong encryption to ensure that your data is protected from prying eyes. This means that your data is transferred and stored in encrypted form so that only you can access it.

## Simple connection and secure transmission

The connection between you and your friends is established in a secure and simple way. If you are in the same network, your computers will find each other automatically. If this is not possible, a special server will help you to establish the connection. Your data is then transferred securely without you having to worry about complicated configuration.

## Your privacy is respected

Crow Backup attaches great importance to protecting your privacy. This means that only the information necessary to secure your backups is stored. The servers on which your data can be temporarily stored are located in Switzerland and are subject to strict data protection laws.

## Conclusion: Your data, your control

To summarise, Crow Backup offers a secure and trustworthy way to back up your important data. Instead of storing your backups in the cloud, you have control over where your data is stored. Your friends will help you store your data safely on their computers, and you can rest assured that your privacy will be respected.

In a world where data is becoming increasingly valuable, it’s reassuring to know that there are ways to keep your information safe and secure. With Crow Backup, you can count on proven security practices and the support of your friends to keep your data in good hands.
