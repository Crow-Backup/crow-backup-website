## Your first backup, together

{{< grid >}}
{{< card icon="computer" >}}
### 1\. Install Crow Backup

You and your friend each [install the app](/en/download/) and create an account.
{{< /card >}}

{{< card icon="connect" >}}
### 2\. Connect and share space

Add your friend using their email address and ask them to accept. If they have not registered yet, Crow Backup sends an invitation. Each of you chooses how much space to share.
{{< /card >}}

{{< card icon="folder" >}}
### 3\. Try a small folder

Choose a folder and your friend as the destination, then start the backup. Keep both computers online. Wait for it to finish, then restore a file to try recovery.
{{< /card >}}
{{< /grid >}}

Once you have tried it, add the folders you want to protect. A large first backup can take longer, depending on your connection and the amount of data. Crow Backup creates subsequent backups automatically when your friend is reachable.

## Have someone in mind? Start together.

Choose a friend or family member with spare disk space. Agree on how much space to offer each other and when you can keep Crow Backup running.

> Want to help each other back up our photos and documents? Crow Backup lets us store encrypted backups on each other’s computers. We each choose how much disk space to share, and we cannot read each other’s files. The software is free. Shall we try it with a small folder first? [https://crowbackup.ch/en/](/en/)

You can install Crow Backup now and invite your friend from the app. Your first backup needs an accepted friendship and an available connection.

## Before you start

{{< faq "Can my friend read my files?" >}}
No. Crow Backup encrypts your files on your computer before sending them. Your friend stores the encrypted data. Crow Backup uses end-to-end encryption (AES-256).
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq "How much disk space do we need?" >}}
You each choose how much space to offer in your friendship settings. Start small, then allow enough space for the files and backup versions you want to keep. Crow Backup does not currently adjust those limits automatically when your disk gets full, so check your available space.
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq "Do both computers need to be online?" >}}
Your friend’s computer needs to be reachable for a backup or restore. You can connect over the internet; you do not need to share a Wi-Fi network. Using the same local network can make a large first backup or restore faster.
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq "What if my friend deletes my backup or stops using Crow Backup?" >}}
That copy may no longer be available for recovery. Agree before removing stored backups or ending a friendship, and keep another independent backup of important files. Ending a friendship in Crow Backup deletes the backups associated with it.
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq "Are Crow Backup servers involved?" >}}
Yes. Crow Backup operates services for accounts and connections. Its servers can also temporarily store encrypted backups when friends are not directly reachable. Your friend receives encrypted files. Read more in the [FAQ](/en/en-faq/).
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq "Why is it free?" >}}
You and your friends provide the storage. Development and running costs are supported by donations. Crow Backup is free and is intended to remain free.
{{< /faq >}}

{{< faq "Where is Crow Backup available?" >}}
Crow Backup can connect over the internet worldwide. For legal reasons, it is currently intended for the Swiss market, as explained in our [FAQ](/en/en-faq/).
{{< /faq >}}

How Crow Backup, cloud backup and local backup compare
| Feature | Crow Backup | Cloud backup | Local backup |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cost | Free software; you and your friends provide storage | Depends on provider and storage plan; recurring fees are common | Requires available storage or a separate drive |
| Privacy | End-to-end encrypted; friends store encrypted files | Encryption and provider access depend on the service | Encryption depends on the software and settings |
| Control | You choose friends, folders and shared storage limits | Features and retention depend on the provider | You manage the device, software and backup schedule |
| Storage location | Friends‘ computers; Crow servers may temporarily store encrypted backups | Provider-operated infrastructure | Wherever you keep the backup device |

Keep another independent copy of important files. With Crow Backup, recovery depends on your backup still being available and your friend being reachable.
